Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version: MoodMate doesn’t collect, send or sell any of your data. Your mood entries and cycle dates are saved only on your device, and I can’t see them.

Who we are

MoodMate is developed by [YOUR NAME], based in the United Kingdom. “We”, “us” and “I” in this policy mean the developer of MoodMate.

What the app stores

When you use MoodMate, the app saves the information you enter, such as your daily mood entries and the dates of your cycles. This is stored locally on your device using Apple’s built-in storage. It is never uploaded to any server run by us or anyone else.

What we don’t collect

MoodMate has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools. We don’t collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers or usage data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone using iCloud or a computer, your MoodMate data may be included in that backup, as with most apps. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

Deleting your data

Because your data only exists on your device, you’re in full control of it. Deleting MoodMate removes all of its data from your device.

Health information

Cycle and mood information can be sensitive. Because it never leaves your device, it is not shared with us, advertisers, data brokers or any other third party.

Children

MoodMate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If MoodMate’s features change in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
